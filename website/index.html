Asked by owners like you.

How accurate is the AI layout reader?

On a real 107-plot drawing it read all 107 plot numbers and placed 103 boxes correctly first time; the other 4 needed a small nudge. You always review the result in the editor before saving, so nothing goes live unchecked.

Where does the 3D view come from?

From the layout you upload. Plotskart raises every plot from the drawing, colours it by status and puts a house on sold plots. For apartments it builds each tower from its floors and flats. Your team can turn, zoom and tap any plot or flat.

Does it work for apartments?

Yes. Add towers, floors and unit types and every flat is created for you, with a stacking plan, a 3D view, floor plans per unit type and prices that include floor rise, preferred location charges and car parks.

Does it work on phones?

Yes. Plotskart works on Android phones, iPhones and any computer browser. Sales staff use it at the site; the owner and accountant at the office.

Can my sales team or brokers see my accounts?

No. Finance, bank accounts, salaries and other people's commission are only visible to the owner and the accountant. Brokers see layouts and their own plots.

How do customers get reminders and receipts?

On Team and Business, booking confirmations, instalment reminders and registration reminders go to the customer on WhatsApp and SMS. On every plan, each payment gets a numbered receipt you can print or share on WhatsApp yourself.

What if I already have plots sold?

Upload the layout, then mark existing bookings and sales with their customers and payments. We help you load this during setup.

Can I start free and upgrade later?

Yes. Your projects, plots, customers and accounts stay exactly as they are. Upgrading adds team logins, more projects and customer reminders on WhatsApp and SMS.

What happens when I reach my monthly limit?

Alerts in the app and on phones never stop. Customer reminders and AI layout reads pause until the next month, or we add more for you: ₹1 per reminder and ₹99 per layout read.

Are prices with GST?

No. 18% GST is added to the setup fee and the subscription. Paying for a full year upfront takes 20% off the monthly price.
